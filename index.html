I build software at the intersection of machine learning, agent infrastructure and blockchain. Since December 2025 that has meant Cruq AI, which I cofounded and where I am CTO.

Before it I was CTO at Sendit Markets, and spent two years at Persistence Labs on liquid staking, money markets and decentralised exchanges. Further back: packet processing at Intel, machine learning research at the Centre for Neuroscience, an MTech in computer science at IISc — and, before any of the engineering, two years selling prepaid telecom across Mumbai and Kolkata, which taught me more about how a product actually reaches people than anything since.
